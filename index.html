METHODOLOGY

How PriceAtlas knows.

01 · SOURCE

Official reference

The current bundled official snapshot is a dated Department of Consumer Affairs Price Monitoring System observation. It is not a quote for every local shop.

02 · LOCAL

Community evidence

Local observations remain separate and are only surfaced after verification.

03 · MATH

Deterministic analysis

Normalization, basket totals, MRP checks and historical statistics are computed directly from structured data.

04 · UNKNOWN

Uncertainty is visible

Missing, stale or conflicting evidence is not turned into a fabricated live price.
